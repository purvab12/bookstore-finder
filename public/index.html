    For booksellers

    Your shop, your events, no fee.

    bookloop is a free listing and event calendar for independent bookshops. Claim your shop, publish readings and book clubs, and meet readers who already like the books you stock.

    • Claim your listing so hours, links and vibes are right.
    • Post an event in under a minute. It appears after a quick review.
    • Keep your data — listings stay yours, with a source link on every record.
    Find your shop Post an event

    We never charge bookshops for a listing or for posting events.

    ✳

    How the data works

    Bookshops come from OpenStreetMap, the open map anyone can correct. Events are posted by bookshops and volunteers, each with a link back to the original listing. Vibe tags are added by readers or claimed by the shop, and every tag shows where it came from. Spot something wrong? Open a shop and choose “I run this shop” to send a correction.
